vijay

Chandrasekaran Joseph Vijay was born on 22 June 1974 in Madras, at the Government Women and Children’s Hospital in Egmore.[4][5][6] His father, Chandrasekaran, is a film director and his mother, Shoba, is a playback singer and vocalist.[7] His father is a lapsed Catholic and his mother is an adherent of Hinduism.[8][9] Vijay had a sister, Vidhya, who died when she was two years old.[10] Vijay did his schooling initially at Fathima school, Kodambakkam and later at Balalok school, Virugambakkam.[11][12] He pursued a bachelor's degree in visual communication from Loyola College, but dropped out early to focus on his acting career.
